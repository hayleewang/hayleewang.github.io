    About “track record.” This dataset records what each animal predicted each year, not what the weather actually did, so it can’t score accuracy. The track record here is each predictor’s history: how many seasons it has called, its split between winter and early spring, and how often it sided with the majority of all predictors that year (a measure of how mainstream its calls are, not of whether it was right). Data: predictions.csv.
